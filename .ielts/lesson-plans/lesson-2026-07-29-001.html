Mars Exploration Milestones

The exploration of Mars has been a significant focus of space agencies worldwide. NASA's Perseverance rover successfully landed on Mars in February 2021, carrying with it the Ingenuity helicopter, which made history as the first powered aircraft to fly on another planet. The journey from Earth to Mars took approximately seven months, covering a distance of about 480 million kilometers. Perseverance's primary mission is to search for signs of ancient microbial life, focusing its efforts on the Jezero Crater, a site believed to have been a lake billions of years ago.

As of 2024, Perseverance had collected 23 rock core samples from the Martian surface, each carefully sealed in titanium tubes for potential future return to Earth. The total cost of the Perseverance mission was approximately $2.7 billion. Meanwhile, China's Tianwen-1 mission, which arrived at Mars in the same month as Perseverance, successfully deployed the Zhurong rover, making China the third nation to land on Mars after the United States and the Soviet Union. India's Mars Orbiter Mission, also known as Mangalyaan, was notable for its remarkably low budget of approximately $74 million, making it one of the most cost-effective interplanetary missions ever attempted.
