## Writing — Four Dimensions

### Task Response (TR)
- 9: Fully addresses all parts, well-developed position, relevant extended ideas
- 8: Sufficiently addresses all parts, clear position, well-supported ideas
- 7: Addresses all parts, clear position throughout, main ideas extended
- 6: Addresses all parts though some fuller than others, clear position, relevant ideas
- 5: Addresses the task only partially, position may be unclear, some ideas irrelevant
- 4: Responds to task only minimally, position unclear, ideas repetitive

### Coherence & Cohesion (CC)
- 9: Manages cohesion skillfully, paragraphing is logical
- 8: Sequences information logically, manages cohesion well, appropriate paragraphing
- 7: Logically organizes information, clear progression, cohesive devices varied
- 6: Arranges information coherently, uses cohesive devices, paragraphing present but may be mechanical
- 5: Presents information with some organization, inadequate/inaccurate cohesive devices
- 4: Information not coherent, basic cohesive devices only, no paragraphing

### Lexical Resource (LR)
- 9: Wide vocabulary with natural control, precise meaning, idiomatic
- 8: Wide vocabulary fluently, conveys precise meaning, occasional inaccuracies
- 7: Sufficient vocabulary for variety, some less common items, occasional errors
- 6: Adequate vocabulary for the task, attempts less common items, some spelling errors
- 5: Limited vocabulary, adequate for the task, noticeable spelling errors
- 4: Basic vocabulary, repetitive, spelling errors cause difficulty

### Grammatical Range & Accuracy (GRA)
- 9: Wide range of structures, flexible and accurate, rare minor errors
- 8: Wide range of structures, majority error-free, occasional inappropriacies
- 7: Variety of complex structures, frequent error-free sentences, good control
- 6: Mix of simple and complex, errors present but don't reduce clarity
- 5: Limited range of structures, errors frequent, some punctuation issues
- 4: Very limited range, subordinate clauses rare, errors predominate

## Speaking — Four Dimensions

### Fluency & Coherence
- 9: Speaks fluently with rare hesitation, any hesitation is content-related, develops topics coherently
- 8: Speaks fluently with occasional repetition/self-correction, hesitation is content-related, develops topics coherently
- 7: Speaks at length without noticeable effort, may demonstrate language-related hesitation, uses connectives
- 6: Willing to speak at length, noticeable repetition and self-correction, uses some cohesive devices
- 5: Maintains flow but noticeable hesitation, limited connectives, simple speech

### Lexical Resource
- 9: Uses vocabulary with full flexibility, idiomatic language naturally
- 8: Wide resource readily, paraphrases effectively, conveys meaning skillfully
- 7: Flexible use of vocabulary, uses some less common items, effective paraphrasing
- 6: Wide enough vocabulary to discuss topics at length, attempts paraphrasing
- 5: Manages talk about familiar topics, limited vocabulary for unfamiliar topics

### Grammatical Range & Accuracy
- 9: Full range of structures, consistently accurate
- 8: Wide range, flexible use, high proportion of error-free sentences
- 7: Range of complex structures, frequently error-free, some grammatical mistakes persist
- 6: Mix of simple and complex structures, errors may be frequent but meaning is clear
- 5: Basic sentence forms, errors are frequent and may cause comprehension difficulty

### Pronunciation
- 9: Uses full range of features with precision, effortless to understand
- 8: Wide range of features, easy to understand throughout, accent has minimal effect
- 7: Displays features of Band 6 and some of Band 8
- 6: Generally intelligible, some mispronunciation of individual words, accent may affect clarity
- 5: Generally intelligible, but mispronunciations cause occasional strain for the listener

## Error Type Taxonomy

### Listening Errors
- `spelling` — heard correctly, spelled wrong
- `number-date` — 15 vs 50, date format confusion
- `missed` — didn't hear the key information
- `no-reaction` — heard but didn't recognize the word/phrase
- `distractor` — misled by a similar-sounding option
- `format` — right answer, wrong format (word limit, capitalization)
- `plural` — missing/extra 's'

### Reading Errors
- `T/F/NG` — confusion distinguishing False vs Not Given
- `headings` — paragraph heading mismatch
- `sentence-completion` — wrong word or word limit exceeded
- `MC` — multiple choice distractor trap
- `matching` — information/person/date matching error
- `synonym` — didn't recognize paraphrase of the passage
- `inference` — required inference not made
